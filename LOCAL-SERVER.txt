To use the project upload form:

1. Double-click start-site.command and leave its Terminal window open.
2. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000/projects.html in your browser.
3. Click the logo, enter the project password, then add the name, JPG thumbnail, and PDF.

Uploads are saved in projects/thumbnails and projects/presentations. Added projects are recorded in projects/projects.json and appear on the Projects page.
The local server listens only on this computer at 127.0.0.1.
